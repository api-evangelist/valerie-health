# Valerie Health

> Valerie Health is an AI-powered "front office" platform for independent healthcare provider groups. It automates referral management, fax and document classification/data extraction, appointment scheduling, and growth analytics, integrating with existing EMR and practice management systems. The company raised a $30M Series A (investors include General Catalyst and Redpoint Ventures) and operates as a HIPAA Business Associate for its Covered Entity customers. No public developer portal or API documentation is published.
