# Valerie Health

Valerie Health is an AI-powered front office platform for independent healthcare provider groups, automating referral management, fax and document processing, appointment scheduling, and growth analytics on top of existing EMR and practice management systems. The company operates as a HIPAA Business Associate and publishes a Vanta-hosted trust center, but no public developer portal or API documentation.
